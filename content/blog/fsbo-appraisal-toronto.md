> **Quick answer:** If you're selling privately, an independent **pre-listing appraisal** gives you a defensible market value based on recent comparable sales. It helps you avoid underpricing (leaving money on the table) or overpricing (sitting unsold), gives buyers documented support for your asking price, and reduces the risk of the buyer's lender appraising below your deal.

## Why pricing is harder without an agent

Agents bring comparable sales and pricing strategy. Without one, many private sellers rely on online estimates or what a neighbour listed for — both can be far off in the GTA's block-by-block market.

## What a pre-listing appraisal gives you

- **A supported market value** from a designated, independent appraiser
- **The comparable sales** and adjustments behind it
- **Confidence in negotiations** — you can show buyers why the price is fair
- **Fewer financing surprises** — if the buyer's lender appraises, your price is already grounded in evidence

## Pricing strategy versus value

The appraisal tells you what the evidence supports. Your **list price** is a strategy decision:

| Strategy | When it can work |
|---|---|
| List at market value | Balanced markets, buyers who want certainty |
| List slightly below to attract offers | Hot markets with multiple buyers |
| List slightly above to leave room to negotiate | Slower markets — but risky if overdone |

## Other things private sellers need

- A real estate lawyer to prepare or review the agreement and handle closing
- Accurate disclosure of known material defects
- Good photos and a clear floor plan
- A plan for showings and safety
- Knowledge of your property's legal status (permits, suites, encroachments)

## Frequently asked questions

### Is an appraisal the same as a CMA?
No. A CMA is a marketing estimate from an agent. An appraisal is an independent opinion of value under professional standards. See [appraisal vs. MPAC vs. CMA](/blog/appraisal-vs-mpac-assessment-vs-cma).

### Should I show the appraisal to buyers?
Many private sellers share it, or summarise its comparable sales, to support their price. It's your choice.

### Will the buyer's bank use my appraisal?
Usually not — lenders order their own. But a well-supported price makes a low lender appraisal less likely.
