> **Quick answer:** The on-site inspection usually takes **30–60 minutes**. Most residential reports are delivered within **2–3 business days** of the inspection, and rush service can be faster. Retrospective, complex or court-bound assignments take longer because of the extra research and detail.

## Typical timeline

| Stage | Typical time |
|---|---|
| Quote and booking | Same day to a few days |
| Site inspection | 30–60 minutes (longer for large or multi-unit homes) |
| Research and analysis | 1–2 days |
| Report writing and review | Included in the delivery window |
| **Total after inspection** | **About 48–72 hours for most homes** |

## What happens during the inspection

The appraiser will:

- Measure the exterior to calculate living area
- Walk through each level and note layout, finishes and condition
- Photograph rooms, mechanicals, exterior and street views
- Note upgrades, defects and anything unusual
- Ask you about renovations, permits and recent repairs

## What slows things down

- **Missing access** to rooms, basements or outbuildings
- **Unclear history** — renovations without dates or permits
- **Retrospective dates**, which need historical sales and evidence of past condition
- **Unique properties** with few comparable sales (rural acreage, heritage homes, very high-end)
- **Multiple parties** — lawyers or co-owners who must approve the scope
- **Peak seasons**, especially spring

## How to speed it up

1. Book early and share your deadline up front.
2. Send documents (survey, renovation list, permits) before the visit.
3. Make sure every room is accessible.
4. Confirm who the report should be addressed to before it's written.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I get a same-day appraisal?
Sometimes, depending on the property and schedule. Ask about rush service when you call.

### Does a condo appraisal take less time?
Often. Strong in-building sales data can make the research quicker.

### How long is an appraisal valid?
An appraisal reflects value on its effective date. Lenders often accept reports for a few months, but in a moving market or for a new purpose you may need an update.
