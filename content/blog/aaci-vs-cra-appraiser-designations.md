> **Quick answer:** The Appraisal Institute of Canada (AIC) grants two professional designations. **CRA** (Canadian Residential Appraiser) members specialise in residential property. **AACI** (Accredited Appraiser Canadian Institute) members can appraise all property types, including commercial. Both use the **P.App** (Professional Appraiser) mark. For a home appraisal, either designation is appropriate.

> *Note: the "CRA" appraiser designation is unrelated to the Canada Revenue Agency.*

## The two designations

| | CRA, P.App | AACI, P.App |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Residential (typically 1–4 units) | All property types |
| Typical clients | Homeowners, lenders, lawyers, executors | Same, plus commercial, industrial, land |
| Education | Degree plus AIC program | Degree plus broader AIC program |
| Standards | CUSPAP | CUSPAP |

Both require education, supervised experience, an ethics commitment, continuing professional development and professional liability insurance.

## Candidates and AIC members

Appraisers working toward a designation are often **candidate members**. Their reports are usually reviewed and co-signed by a designated member. That's normal — just know who is signing your report.

## Why it matters

- Lenders, courts and the CRA expect valuations from qualified, accountable professionals
- Designated members follow **CUSPAP**, the Canadian Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice
- There's a complaint and discipline process if something goes wrong

## How to check an appraiser

1. Ask for their designation and AIC member number.
2. Look them up in the AIC's public member directory.
3. Confirm they carry professional liability insurance.
4. Ask whether they regularly appraise your property type and area.

## When an AACI might be better

For residential properties with unusual features — rural acreage with outbuildings, mixed-use buildings, development land, or properties with commercial components — an AACI's broader scope can help.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a real estate agent qualified to appraise my home?
Agents can give opinions of price for marketing, but a formal appraisal for lending, legal or tax use should come from a qualified appraiser.

### Does P.App mean the same as AACI?
P.App is the professional mark used by members holding either designation.

### Does every appraiser in Ontario need to be AIC-designated?
Many lenders and legal users require designated appraisers. Ask your lender, lawyer or accountant what they accept.
