> **Quick answer:** Multiplexes are valued using **comparable sales** of similar income properties and the **income approach** — how much rent the units produce and how buyers price that income. Toronto's as-of-right permissions for up to four units in many low-rise zones have increased interest in conversions, but value depends on the units that legally exist and their income, not just what zoning allows.

## What's changed

Toronto now permits duplexes, triplexes and fourplexes as-of-right in many residential zones, and existing houses can be converted to up to four units, subject to building code, fire separation and other requirements. Garden or laneway suites may also be possible on qualifying lots.

## Two lenses on value

### Sales comparison
How much did similar multiplexes in the area sell for — adjusted for number of units, unit sizes, condition, parking and lot?

### Income approach
- **Gross rents** — actual leases and market rents
- **Vacancy and expenses** — taxes, insurance, utilities, maintenance
- **Net operating income**
- **Capitalisation rates** from comparable investment sales

Where existing tenancies are below market, appraisers consider current rents and Ontario's rent rules, which affect how quickly rents can change.

## Conversion projects

For a house being converted, appraisers may provide:

- **As-is value** — today, before conversion
- **As-complete value** — once units are built and legal (with stated assumptions)

Lenders financing conversions often want both.

## What lenders look for

- Legal status of each unit (permits and inspections)
- Signed leases and rent rolls
- Separate entrances, utilities and fire separation
- Realistic expense estimates

## Frequently asked questions

### Does as-of-right zoning automatically raise my home's value?
Not automatically. Buyers pay for proven income and completed legal units. Potential may add some value where the market clearly prices it in.

### Are multiplex appraisals more expensive?
Generally, yes — they require income analysis and more documentation.

### Can you appraise a property that's mid-conversion?
Yes. The report typically gives as-is and as-complete values with clear assumptions.
