> **Quick answer:** As an **attorney for property** in Ontario, you have a duty to manage your parent's assets in their best interest and keep accounts. If you sell the home, an independent appraisal is strong evidence that you sold at **fair market value** — protecting your parent, protecting you from accusations, and reducing family conflict.

## Your responsibilities as attorney (in brief)

- Act honestly, in good faith and for the parent's benefit
- Keep accurate records of decisions and transactions
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Be prepared to account to the parent, other family members or the court

*This is general information — your lawyer can explain your duties in detail.*

## Why an appraisal helps

- **Sets a fair price** before listing or accepting an offer
- **Documents your process** if a sibling later questions the sale
- **Supports family sales** — if a relative is buying, an independent value is essential
- **Helps plan care funding** — knowing the value informs long-term care or retirement home decisions

## Selling to a family member

If you or another relative is buying the home, the conflict-of-interest risk is high. An independent appraisal (and independent legal advice) is the minimum protection. Selling below market value could be challenged later — including by the estate.

## Steps that work

1. Confirm the power of attorney is valid and covers the sale.
2. Get an independent appraisal.
3. Discuss the plan with family members where appropriate.
4. List or negotiate with the appraisal as your guide.
5. Keep copies of everything with your attorney accounts.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need court approval to sell?
Not usually under a valid continuing power of attorney for property, but get legal advice about your specific document.

### Can the appraisal be used later for the estate?
It's prepared for a different date and purpose. The estate will likely need a date-of-death value if your parent passes away.

### What if my siblings disagree with the price?
An independent, well-supported appraisal is the best way to show the price was fair.
