> **Quick answer:** Ontario's Estate Administration Tax (often called probate fees) is **nothing on the first $50,000** of an estate's value and **$15 per $1,000 (1.5%)** above that. Real estate is included at **fair market value at the date of death**, reduced only by mortgages registered against that property. Other debts — credit cards, lines of credit not secured on the home — don't reduce the value for this tax.

## How it's calculated

| Estate value passing through the will | Tax |
|---|---|
| First $50,000 | $0 |
| Each $1,000 above $50,000 | $15 |

### Example 1: a house with a mortgage
- Home value at date of death: $1,000,000
- Registered mortgage: $300,000
- Other assets (bank, investments): $100,000
- Value for tax: $1,000,000 − $300,000 + $100,000 = **$800,000**
- Tax: ($800,000 − $50,000) × 1.5% = **$11,250**

### Example 2: same estate, no mortgage
- Value for tax: $1,100,000
- Tax: ($1,100,000 − $50,000) × 1.5% = **$15,750**

## What's usually outside the estate

- Property held in **joint tenancy** with right of survivorship (generally passes directly to the survivor)
- Assets with **named beneficiaries** (many RRSPs, RRIFs, TFSAs, life insurance)
- Assets held in some trusts

Planning around probate has trade-offs — talk to an estate lawyer before changing ownership.

## Why the home's value matters

The house is often the largest asset. Overstating it increases tax; understating it can trigger reassessment and penalties. After the certificate is issued, executors must file an **Estate Information Return** within 180 days, listing the assets and their values. A date-of-death appraisal is solid support for that filing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Estate Administration Tax the same as income tax at death?
No. It's a provincial tax on the estate's value. Separately, the CRA taxes capital gains on the deceased's final return.

### Can I use the MPAC assessment for the home's value?
It's based on 2016 values and isn't market value at the date of death. An appraisal is more reliable.

### What if values change after death?
Estate Administration Tax uses the value at the date of death. Later changes affect the estate's own tax position when it sells.
