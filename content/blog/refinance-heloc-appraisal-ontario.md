> **Quick answer:** To refinance or open a home equity line of credit (HELOC), your lender needs an independent value to calculate how much it can lend. In Canada, a refinance from a federally regulated lender is generally capped at **80% of the home's value**, and the revolving HELOC portion at **65%**, minus what you already owe. A careful appraisal that captures every upgrade can make a real difference to how much equity you can access.

## How the value turns into borrowing room

| | Example |
|---|---|
| Appraised value | $1,100,000 |
| 80% maximum | $880,000 |
| Current mortgage | − $520,000 |
| **Approximate room** | **$360,000** (subject to income qualification) |

A $50,000 difference in value changes the available amount by $40,000 at 80% loan-to-value. That's why preparation matters.

## Who orders the appraisal?

Usually the **lender** or your **mortgage broker**, through an approved panel. Some lenders accept reports from designated appraisers you hire directly — ask first. Either way, the report must be addressed to the lender to be used for lending.

## How to prepare

- List renovations with dates and costs: kitchen, baths, roof, windows, HVAC, basement
- Have permits ready for additions and secondary suites
- Share rental income for legal units (lease, rent)
- Make every room and the basement accessible
- Tell your broker about recent nearby sales you know of

See the full [appraisal preparation checklist](/blog/how-to-prepare-for-home-appraisal).

## When an independent appraisal still helps

- **Private and alternative lenders** often accept or require an independent report addressed to them
- **Planning** — knowing your number before applying avoids surprises
- **Reconsiderations** — evidence to support a request if the lender's value seems low

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use last year's appraisal?
Most lenders want a recent valuation. Ask your lender how old a report can be.

### What if the appraisal comes in lower than expected?
Ask your broker about a reconsideration with better comparable sales, or whether another lender would order a new appraisal. See [what to do about a low appraisal](/blog/low-home-appraisal-what-to-do).

### Does a HELOC always need a full interior appraisal?
Not always. Some lenders use automated or desktop valuations for low-risk files. Others require a full inspection.
