> **Quick answer:** When a pre-construction condo closes, your lender appraises the unit at **today's market value**, which may differ from the price you agreed years earlier. If it's lower, you may need extra cash to close. For **assignment sales**, appraisers look at comparable resales and assignments in similar new buildings, adjusting for the unit's features and the expected closing date.

## Why closing appraisals surprise buyers

- You signed years ago, often in a stronger market
- Similar resale units nearby may now be selling for less
- New buildings may have many investors listing at once
- Lenders lend on the **lower** of price or appraised value

## How appraisers value an unfinished or newly finished unit

1. **Comparable resales** in similar, recently completed buildings
2. **Assignment sales** in the same or similar projects
3. **Builder's current pricing** (with caution — includes incentives and marketing)
4. **Adjustments** for size, floor, view, exposure, parking, locker and finishes

## If the closing appraisal is short

Options may include:

- Adding cash to close the gap
- Trying another lender (via a mortgage broker)
- Builder or alternative lender financing
- Assigning the unit before closing (if your agreement allows)

Talk to your broker and lawyer early — closing timelines are tight.

## Assignment sales

When you sell your contract to someone else:

- The buyer's lender values the unit as of the expected closing
- Your agreement may require builder consent and fees
- Pricing must account for deposits already paid

An independent appraisal helps you set a realistic assignment price.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I get an appraisal before occupancy?
Yes. Appraisers can value based on plans and specifications, with clear assumptions about completion.

### Does interim occupancy affect value?
Occupancy fees and unregistered status affect financing and timing, not usually the unit's market value itself.

### What if my building has very few sales?
Appraisers rely more on similar nearby buildings and explain the comparability.
