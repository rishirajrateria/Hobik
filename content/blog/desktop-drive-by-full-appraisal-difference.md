> **Quick answer:** A **full appraisal** includes an interior and exterior inspection and is the most reliable. A **drive-by (exterior-only)** appraisal relies on an exterior view plus records and assumptions about the interior. A **desktop** appraisal uses data, photos and records with no physical inspection. The right choice depends on who will rely on the report and how much is at stake.

## Comparison

| | Full | Drive-by / exterior | Desktop |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interior inspection | Yes | No | No |
| Exterior inspection | Yes | Yes | No (photos/data) |
| Reliability | Highest | Moderate | Depends on data quality |
| Speed | Standard | Faster | Fastest |
| Typical use | Lending, estates, divorce, court, CRA | Some lending, access-limited files, retrospective work | Portfolio reviews, early planning, some lender products |

## When less inspection is acceptable

- **Access isn't possible** — a tenant or estranged spouse won't allow entry
- **Retrospective appraisals** where the interior has since changed
- **Low-risk lending** where the lender's policy allows it
- **Preliminary planning** before a full appraisal

## When you should insist on a full appraisal

- Court or family-law matters where the report may be challenged
- Large tax consequences (estate, change in use)
- Unique or heavily renovated properties
- Properties with secondary suites or significant defects

## How limited inspections are reported

A good report states clearly what was **not** inspected and the **extraordinary assumptions** made (for example, "the interior is assumed to be in average condition"). If those assumptions turn out to be wrong, the value may change.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a desktop appraisal the same as an online estimate?
No. A desktop appraisal is prepared and signed by an appraiser under professional standards. Online estimates are automated and unsigned.

### Can I upgrade a drive-by to a full appraisal later?
Yes — the appraiser can inspect the interior and update the report.

### Are limited appraisals cheaper?
Often a bit, but the research and analysis are still most of the work.
