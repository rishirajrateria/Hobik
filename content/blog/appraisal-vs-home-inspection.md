> **Quick answer:** An **appraisal** estimates market value using comparable sales. A **home inspection** examines the home's condition and systems to find defects. Buyers usually need both: the inspection protects you from hidden problems; the appraisal protects you (and your lender) from overpaying.

## Side by side

| | Appraisal | Home inspection |
|---|---|---|
| Question answered | What is it worth? | What condition is it in? |
| Who usually orders | Lender, buyer, owner, lawyer | Buyer |
| Main output | Market value with supporting sales | List of defects and maintenance items |
| Looks at | Size, layout, condition, features, location, market | Roof, structure, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, moisture |
| Time on site | 30–60 minutes | 2–4 hours |
| Opens panels / tests systems | No | Yes (visual and non-invasive) |

## How they affect each other

- A serious defect found in an inspection (foundation cracks, roof at end of life, old wiring) can reduce what a home is worth.
- An appraiser notes visible condition, but won't climb on the roof or test the furnace. If the appraiser isn't told about a problem, the value may assume it doesn't exist.

## When you need each

| Situation | Appraisal | Inspection |
|---|---|---|
| Buying with a mortgage | ✔ (lender) | ✔ (strongly recommended) |
| Buying firm in a bidding war | ✔ (pre-purchase) | ✔ (pre-offer, if possible) |
| Refinancing | ✔ | — |
| Estate or divorce | ✔ | Sometimes, if condition is disputed |
| Selling | Pre-listing appraisal (optional) | Pre-listing inspection (optional) |

## Frequently asked questions

### Can one person do both?
They're different professions with different training. Some people hold both qualifications, but the reports remain separate.

### Should I share my inspection report with the appraiser?
If you're the client and condition matters, yes — it helps the appraiser reflect issues accurately.

### Does a good inspection guarantee a good appraisal?
No. A home can be in perfect condition and still appraise below the price if comparable sales don't support it.
